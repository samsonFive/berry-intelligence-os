Regina blackberry

Variety and commercial profile · 13 November 2026

Regina is a blackberry promoted by Black Venture Farm (BVF), part of Frutura’s Sun Belle berry business, with reported cultivation in Michoacán, Mexico. Public communications describe floricane fruiting and emphasize fruit quality; formal cultivar identity, rights, measured performance, and commercial scale remain unresolved. 1,2,3,4,5,7

  • Public footprint: BVF’s 29 April 2026 field publication names Regina and Aketzali in Tocumbo. A separate plant offer includes Regina; neither establishes a launch date, acreage, or current stock. 1,2
  • A separate raspberry bears the same name. Regina records associated with Ilario Ioriatti and Berryplant concern raspberry and do not establish the blackberry’s identity or traits. 14

An integrated commercial platform

Black Venture Farm combines breeding, propagation, and grower support within Frutura’s integrated Sun Belle berry business.

The Giddings acquisition was announced in October 2023 and completed in 2024. Frutura’s sustainability report dates Giddings’ formal entry into the group to October 2024. At announcement, BVF was Giddings’ wholly owned Mexican breeding subsidiary. 6,8,15

In December 2024, Frutura consolidated its berry operations under the Sun Belle brand and renamed the Mexican operations Sun Belle Mexico. Contemporary coverage identifies BVF as a wholly owned subsidiary of Sun Belle Mexico. The current Frutura portfolio confirms BVF’s breeding/propagation role and Sun Belle’s marketing role. 5,7

Identity, breeding, and rights

Regina is documented as a commercial name; its legal denomination or selection code has not been established. An industry post attributes development/ownership to BVF. Breeding location and country of origin, parentage, selection history, and introduction date are undocumented in the reviewed material. Mexican cultivation does not by itself establish Mexican breeding origin. 2,4

An exact-name CPVO search on 12 November 2026 returned eight records and no blackberry match; its only Rubus result was raspberry. This search does not exclude protection under another denomination or in another jurisdiction. Regina-specific patents, variety rights, license territories, royalties, and exclusivity remain unidentified. 16

Aketzali holds an issued USDA plant variety protection certificate, dated 4 June 2021 (certificate 202000246). This documents protection for another BVF blackberry; it does not identify Regina’s rights. 9
