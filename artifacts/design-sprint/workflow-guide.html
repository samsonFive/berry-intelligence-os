What has happened, and what should I read?

Scan recent coverage, read the article, save useful stories and return to your reading list.

How it works & what changes

Keep origin labels without duplicate articles; preserve saved marks, reading priority and unread/in-progress/completed state. Keep date, berry, country and company scope. 02 · How do I work?

Read freely. Investigate selectively. Review deliberately.

You do not need to turn every article into a formal intelligence record. These are related paths, with deliberate handoffs where deeper work is useful.

Your everyday loop
  1. 01 · SCAN

    See what is new

    Open News. Set berry, country, company and date scope. Newest publication first.

  2. 02 · READ

    Understand the story

    Read in the overlay or open the publisher. Save it or mark usefulness without changing trust.

  3. 03 · INVESTIGATE

    Follow the context

    Open Companies, Varieties, Map or Learn. Check source dates, linked statements and missing coverage.

  4. 04 · RETURN

    Keep a useful watch

    Choose what to track. Inspect relevant changes, revisit saved items and return to News.

When a finding needs formal analysis
  1. A · FRAME

    Ask a specific question

    Define company, crop, geography and period. Gather relevant sources and identify what is missing.

  2. B · EXAMINE

    Check the evidence

    Inspect exact wording, scope, source identity and independence. Reprints do not count as separate corroboration.

  3. C · INTERPRET

    Explain what follows

    Use reviewed findings where appropriate. Keep patterns, analyst assessments and recommended actions labeled separately.

Human review · separate decisions

Source → publication review

Decide whether the source publication enters the published collection. Authenticity checks answer whether recovered text belongs to the right source.

Publishing an article does not confirm every claim it contains.

Human review · separate decisions

Proposition or pattern → its own review

Review individual propositions and proposed patterns in their existing tools, with supporting evidence and scope visible.

A testing Pass, feed statement decision or candidate confirmation is not automatic creation of a canonical Fact or Signal.

Then communicate: select the appropriate brief or report, check its sources and caveats, present or export, and retain follow-up questions in your watches or research agenda.

No automatic trust ladder. These arrows describe an analyst's work, not an automatic conversion of articles into Facts, Signals or Assessments. AI drafts, educational material and reader feedback do not approve intelligence.

04 · What is proven?

A working page is only the first check.

The audit covers 58 navigation entries and 165 registered routes. Its 79 isolated render checks found 72 successful pages, two expected redirects, four expected permission denials and one broken Fact-shaped link. The four restricted pages also opened successfully in separate authoring-mode checks within that total.

Inspected

Code and templates establish intended behavior. An implementation can still be incomplete or misleading.

Page checked

The specified page opens with committed sample data. This does not prove its actions, private data or live providers work.

Workflow verified

A named job succeeds from start to finish, with saved state, permissions and output checked. Record the environment and date.

Preview / proposed

A demonstrated interaction may use temporary browser state. A recommended grouping is not yet implemented navigation.

Every section gets a purpose card

  1. Purpose: whose question does this answer, and when should they use it?
  2. Mechanism: what goes in, what the user does, what comes out, and where it is saved.
  3. Proof: what was actually tested, what failed, and what remains unverified.
  4. Disposition: keep, combine, improve, relocate or retire—with a named replacement and reason.
  5. Preservation: keep source links, history, bookmarks, permissions and distinct decisions.

Prove the jobs before removing pages

  • News → save to Personal Digest → prioritize/read/complete → revisit.
  • Watch a company → see a relevant change → inspect it.
  • Inspect a variety → verify a growing-region source → return.
  • Review a finding → build a brief/report → export and check it.
  • Review a publication and a proposition as separate decisions.
  • Select a topic → research or open a lesson → visual Learn → return to the investigation.

Current status: end-to-end consolidation acceptance remains pending. Low usage and empty test queues do not establish that a feature is abandoned.

Definition of done: this guide must match the delivered navigation and demonstrated workflows at handoff. Keep today's capabilities, preview-only additions, open defects and future proposals visibly separate throughout the work.

Photo seeds stay separate from confirmed varieties.

    Keep the company association

  2. 2 - REVIEW

    Check spelling and identity

    Filter Registry seeds by company, crop and A-Z. Open possible existing matches and use the existing identity review.

    Establish what the source proves
