DESIGN MISSION · WORKING DIRECTION

A bright, immersive workspace
for agricultural intelligence.

Use the whole screen to help an analyst scan, understand and act. Bring green, berry color and translucent materials into a dense, readable daily workspace.

What the audit found

  • The wide navigation becomes a long block above the feed at narrower widths.
  • Multiple shells and card systems create inconsistent hierarchy and spacing.
  • Filters, disclosures and page headers compete with the actual intelligence.
  • The Reader needs distinct headline, source, summary, claim and evidence roles.

What to decide here

  • Visual language: contrast, density, typography, imagery and comfortable reading.
  • Navigation: Learn stays top level; Statements lives under More. Every existing section is preserved in the navigation inventory.
  • Core workflows and explicitly requested features. Working preview controls do not imply production integration.
  • News is the primary experience: scan image-led stories and read in a dedicated pane. Explore retains geographic context.
  • Whether the density is comfortable on your everyday screen.
  • How much color and motion supports long reading sessions.

Build sequence

  1. Choose and refine this visual direction.
  2. Implement the shared shell, navigation, filters and Reader.
  3. Complete scan → select → read → review using existing services.
  4. Migrate companies, varieties, reports, queues and remaining pages in groups.
  5. Verify keyboard use, responsive layouts, contrast and trust boundaries.

Product rules

  • Trusted statements retain their classification and source provenance.
  • Useful feedback prepares candidates; human confirmation remains separate.
  • Counts describe available records, never market share or confidence.
  • Empty coverage and old source dates stay visible.

This is the prototype phase. The complete mission and initial audit are stored in the design branch alongside this study.

ABOUT THIS STUDY

Real content. A design preview.

The sample is drawn from published repository evidence across all four berries, including a fictional patent seed record identified in its summary. Its source dates are shown as stored. It is a curated historical sample, not a live news feed or a measure of market coverage.

Trusted shows sources linked to active canonical facts. Claim labels remain visible. Unreviewed shows the raw source view, including sources that also support trusted statements.

Saving and feedback only change this preview's local state. They do not extract, confirm, publish, or modify application records.

Article images in this study are generated agricultural illustrations, not photographs from the linked sources. Each is labeled. Source text and dates remain real stored records.

Map boundaries use the existing Natural Earth asset and are contextual, not a statement of sovereignty.
