# Recommended next product wave

Build an operator-safe publication review and promotion workflow for body-bearing acquisition drafts.

The evidence does not support another extraction rewrite. The current pipeline already acquires readable bodies from real UF and Fruitist pages and persists honest failure outcomes. The trusted corpus has zero readable bodies because older trusted records predate the pipeline and no newer readable draft has passed mandatory human publication review. Oishii and some Fruitist pages genuinely contain no article body, so extractor changes cannot recover text that publishers do not provide.

The next wave should let an operator:

1. filter review drafts by readable-body state, company, source, freshness, and acquisition outcome;
2. inspect body text, provenance, extractor version, diagnostics, entity links, and duplicate candidates in one view;
3. approve, reject, or request manual acquisition with an explicit human decision and audit trail;
4. promote only approved records into trusted Evidence while preserving publication date and provenance;
5. preview the exact effect on Today, company coverage, and reports before promotion;
6. handle batches safely with selection, reason capture, caps, dry-run summaries, and rollback-safe validation;
7. keep blocked, bodyless, consent, and bot-wall captures out of readable intelligence.

Acceptance should include a small fixture-backed end-to-end review, zero automatic approval, no production-data mutation during tests, and explicit proof that approved body-bearing evidence becomes readable in Today while rejected or unavailable content does not.
