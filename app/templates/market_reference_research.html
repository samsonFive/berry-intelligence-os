What these figures can and cannot show

National crop statistics provide market context. They do not establish a company’s acreage, a variety’s performance or a reviewed statement. Mixed crops, regional totals, export volumes, unavailable values and calculated yield are excluded from this production-reference workflow. Source categories remain separate, with their original units; figures are not summed across sources. Public snapshots use the published dataset and exclude private research and review history.
