Map scope & source notes

Country and berry selections apply to all layers. Company, watchlist and tier selections use stored assignments. Variety company filters use recorded ownership, development, licensing, growing, trial, marketing or distribution roles.

News dates and news review status do not remove long-lived region entries or annual statistics. Observed-by dates exclude later observations and retain undated entries as unknown. Location status describes the activity; a user entry remains unreviewed even when marked active. Missing information is unknown, not zero. Subregions remain labeled notes until boundaries are available. Boundaries are contextual, not a statement of sovereignty.

Market Snapshots include reviewed, escalated source inventory. Market figures can be selected separately in the snapshot composition; their periods, definitions and sources stay attached. Private region notes remain excluded.
