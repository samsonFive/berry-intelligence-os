Coverage & review boundaries

These are public statistical references, separate from reviewed source statements. Saving figures does not approve a claim or overwrite canonical records. Missing values are never filled with zero or an inferred yield. Your selected references carry between Map Explorer and its snapshot composition/export in the analyst workspace. Public snapshots retain the published reference dataset.

The Eurostat check uses the existing keyless agency API. The separate UK check reads the current public Defra workbook and retains its original file and source cell references. Neither check selects figures automatically. Other country/berry research starts only through the separate explicit research request. Opening this page never requests data. Each capture and research response is retained privately for comparison; failures keep the previous references.
