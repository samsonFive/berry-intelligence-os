How to read these fields

References show information available to inspect, not verified identities, independent trait measurements or current legal rights. Multiple references can describe the same filing. Cited traits count source-linked profile entries and retained trait statements; they may include owner claims. Check each profile's attribution. Company roles come from recorded relationship links, not company names on portfolio pages. Permission-held photos remain session-only unless reuse is recorded. This table covers the catalog, not all discovered candidates or every possible profile field.
