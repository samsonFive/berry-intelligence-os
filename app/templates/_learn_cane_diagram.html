Follow the cane, then the fruit.

Floricane-fruiting types grow a cane in its first year and bear fruit on that cane in its second year.

Floricane

Second-year fruiting

A cane is a primocane in year one and a floricane in year two. Fruiting habit and pruning determine whether it carries fruit in either year. The crown produces new canes as old ones finish.

Check your understanding: does “primocane” mean sweeter fruit?

No. It names a cane’s first year of growth. A “primocane-fruiting” variety can fruit in that year; it is not a sweetness or quality grade.
