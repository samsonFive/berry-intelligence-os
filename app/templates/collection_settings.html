What do these columns mean?

Region is the geographic search scope. APAC means Asia-Pacific. Berry shows the configured crop or crop names present in the search; missing scopes stay Not specified.

Workflow is where a search is used: News (previously Today), Industry discovery (Pulse), Weekly edition, Emerging developments (Radar), or Background recall. A search can serve more than one workflow.

Provider is the service that discovers articles: Google News, Exa (web / semantic search), Perplexity, or NewsCatcher CatchAll (background news recall). Optional services depend on credentials and routing; enabled terms do not mean a service is currently running.

Language is the query or search-edition language. Lang previously marked regional-language searches. It does not guarantee the language of returned stories. Names now describe the subject; A–Z follows those names.

How collection works
