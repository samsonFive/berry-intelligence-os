How these filters work
  • Company lists, favorites and tiers use the same personal marks as Companies, News, Map and Digest. Combined personal filters must match the same company.
  • Company and variety country coverage uses active stored operating, growing or trial relationships. A source mentioning a country does not prove a company operates or a variety grows there. Editable Map annotations stay unreviewed and are not used as confirmed Landscape coverage.
  • Source dates use publication dates, newest first. Undated sources remain separate. Portfolios, signals and assessments are standing records and are not filtered by source date.
  • Signals and assessments retain their own review labels. Geography uses explicit scope when provided; otherwise it can follow linked supporting sources. No review status changes here.
  • Photo variety associations awaiting identity review do not establish commercial roles. Existing berry briefings and coverage tools remain available below.

Scope carried into these tools

News carries berries, countries, date range and personal company filters; its source lanes differ. News and Map support one explicit company, so multiple-company and variety selections stay here. Map uses geographic inventory rather than news dates. Report scope carries selected companies, varieties and countries; personal company filters resolve to matching catalog companies. It supports one berry and trailing 7/30-day windows. Multiple berries, custom dates and requested sections appear for review rather than silently changing its standard report format. Brief starts with up to four companies and four varieties from this view; inspect its selection before presenting. The brief does not carry country or custom-date filters.
