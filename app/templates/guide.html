Trusted intelligence lifecycle

  1. Sources — places we watch for public material (company sites, registries, trade press).
  2. Publications — individual articles, filings, or pages DISCOVERED from those Sources.
  3. Publication Review — REVIEW REQUIRED. A human decides whether a Publication may enter the trusted workflow. Rejecting it does not erase the Source.
  4. Evidence — TRUSTED published material after review. This is the corpus later work cites.
  5. Signals — a pattern that may be emerging, supported by Evidence. AI PROPOSED candidates stay untrusted until reviewed.
  6. Assessments — analyst interpretation of Facts. Never auto-created from a Signal.
  7. Strategic Questions — enduring questions you maintain. The system organizes trusted material against them; it does not answer them.
  8. Brief Packs — a presentation composed from existing trusted intelligence. Not a new trust object.{% if not static_build %} Reports, when available, are the same idea: an output, not a review action.{% endif %}

Variety identity (separate from trusted Varieties)

  1. Variety discovery — a public cultivar name appears in a registry import or trusted corpus mention.
  2. Candidate — CANDIDATE. Not a trusted Variety. The Variety index never treats this count as coverage.
  3. Identity Review — decide DISTINCT, POSSIBLE ALIAS, UNKNOWN, or (human only) CONFIRMED SAME.
  4. Trusted Variety — only an existing canonical Variety record. Reviewing a candidate never auto-creates one.

Collection

  1. Collection Ops — status of the scheduled collector. Viewing it never starts a run.
  2. Fresh public material — newly seen pages and filings staged from Sources.
  3. Review queue — those Publications wait in Publication Review until a human decides.

Words this workbench reuses

DISCOVERED
Found by collection. Not yet trusted.
REVIEW REQUIRED
Waiting for a human decision.
TRUSTED
Accepted into the published intelligence corpus.
AI PROPOSED
A machine suggestion. Untrusted until reviewed.
REVIEWED
A human has recorded a decision.
CANDIDATE
Proposed identity or pattern. Not the trusted object.
POSSIBLE ALIAS
May be the same Variety as an existing record. Human must decide.
OPERATOR ACTION
A collection/Source problem for an operator, not a trust decision.
