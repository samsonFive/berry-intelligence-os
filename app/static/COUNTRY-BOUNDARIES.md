# Country boundary attribution

`countries.geojson` is a locally bundled, simplified derivative of Natural Earth
country boundaries distributed by datasets/geo-countries (public domain/ODC PDDL).
Source: https://github.com/datasets/geo-countries
Download: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/datasets/geo-countries/master/data/countries.geojson
Retrieved 2026-09-29. Coordinates simplified to 0.035 degrees and rounded to
three decimals. No runtime tile service, external map library or data upload.
Boundaries are contextual and do not imply a sovereignty determination.
