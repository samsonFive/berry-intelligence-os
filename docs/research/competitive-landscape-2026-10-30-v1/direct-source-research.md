Prior revisions remain intact. `run.json` tracks current revisions. Original source inspection, attribution, dates, unavailable pages and entity-specific gaps remain in the returns.

`python -m scripts.save_direct_research_batch` reads an authored JSON packet on stdin and validates its profiles against the frozen batch membership. It requires entity-specific gaps and a search-pass audit, validates capability/source/canonical relationship references, preserves Unicode and numerical wording, and rejects unexpected table fields. It does not fetch sources, qualify evidence, import application records or produce ratings. Research judgments and source inspection remain separate work.

The inspected sources require several distinctions: genetic access versus ownership, grower-owned marketing partnerships versus their member farms, disease-control robots versus harvesting robots, actual harvest versus trial/expansion plans, and job adverts versus appointments. Undated year-round offers do not establish a defined-year supply-month anchor. Company and supplier assertions retain attribution and are not automatically independent corroboration.
