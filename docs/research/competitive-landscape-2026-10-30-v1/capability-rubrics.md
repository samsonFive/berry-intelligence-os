## Shared confidence and scope contract

| Confidence | Requirements |
| --- | --- |
| high | Direct, recent, scope-matched evidence with adequate method plus independent corroboration; no material unresolved conflict. |
| medium | Direct, scope-matched evidence with adequate detail, but independence or recency is limited; attribute company assertions. |
| low | An anchor is explicitly supported but evidence is older or method/corroboration is limited; state which factor. Missing anchor support means Unknown, not Low plus a number. |

Every assessment stores entity, peer role, crop, destination, program/variety when relevant, period, origin context, rationale, evidence/counterevidence, date, rubric version, confidence and review status. Access/control models are categorical: owned, exclusive or nonexclusive license, public access, commercial supply, mixed, Unknown. Supply coverage bands measure months only; zero remains a raw value outside the five positive bands. Channel categories are grocery retail, foodservice, specialist wholesale, direct to consumer and processing/ingredient. No quantity is a quality ranking.

Nurseries/propagators, buyers, investors, regulators and other support actors stay ecosystem nodes. No extra peer rubric or financial resilience score without comparable evidence and a versioned specification.

Freshness review reminders (days): vacancy_status: 14, current_professional_role: 90, operating_or_market_presence: 365, rights_access_continuity: 365, trial_method_and_outcomes: 1825. These are review reminders, not evidence expiry or automatic rescores. Preserve original effective and observation dates. A historic trial can remain informative, while a job must retain last-observed wording. Context changes or conflicting evidence can trigger review sooner. The people-event research window is 24 months, independent of these reminders.
