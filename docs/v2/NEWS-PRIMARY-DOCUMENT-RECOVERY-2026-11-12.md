# Preserve public-record text through normal article intake

Controlled historical acquisition exposed two parsing failures: a Chilean blueberry crop report was treated as HTML despite being a PDF, and DEFRA's small-fruit statistical table was removed by `include_tables=False`. The existing Reader could extract selectable PDF text, but normal article intake did not call it. DEFRA's precise `govuk:first-published-at` metadata also remained unused. These are observed acquisition losses, not proof that discovery failed.

Article acquisition v5 reuses the Reader's bounded public PDF transport and isolated pypdf subprocess for explicit `.pdf` URLs. It retains original page markers and paragraph locators. Text is marked `content_kind=pdf`, `availability=excerpt_only`; source-body and read-time classification keep it partial even when it is long. It never becomes FULL_ARTICLE completeness automatically. There is no OCR or interpretation of charts, and blocked, empty or truncated captures remain explicit failures. PDF dates, titles and authors remain unknown unless independently supplied as source metadata; filenames and creation times do not fill them.

HTML acquisition now retains the main article's statistical tables through the existing extractor. Their source text and headers remain untrusted text, not interpreted observations or Atomic Evidence. Publication-date extraction accepts the explicit first-publication metadata only on GOV.UK, with the existing conflict handling; modified times remain separate. A source-declared publication timestamp does not prove independently observed first-public visibility.

The two real PDF controls (Agreste's September strawberry report and Chile's week-43 blueberry report) now produce bounded selectable text. The current DEFRA HTML control retains its raspberry table and original September 24 publication day. The earlier failed acquisition artifacts remain frozen. These are known-URL engineering controls, not independent prospective recall wins or proof of comprehensive archives.

Source-reviewed historical intake also exposed Spanish metadata misses for plural export wording, the World Raspberry Congress and season revisions. Shared regional vocabulary recognizes exportaciones/importaciones, campaña/campañas, harvest/yield plurals, labor and specific world/international congress phrases. Original-language metadata determines relevance; English presentation does not manufacture a crop subject. Existing recipe, software, cosmetic and other-crop controls remain.

Normal URL identity and duplicate checks are unchanged. Existing drafts, human review states and original bodies are not migrated or rewritten. Reconstructed earlier acquisitions can preserve their original acquisition version explicitly; they must not claim a fresh v5 fetch. Publication review and Atomic Evidence review remain mandatory human gates. No Source registry approval, provider call, model installation or recurring pipeline activation is added.

Remaining gaps: non-suffix PDF download endpoints, scans and figures, complete public-record archives, source-body discovery when crop identity is absent from feed metadata, unsafe/unsupported English presentation, and independent pilot event/latency review. The AP acquisition remains blocked; the FDA outbreak page has conflicting date metadata; the Chilean PDF's filename does not establish its publication time. Preserve those limits. Owners: News acquisition engineering, source-language review and independent coverage assessment.

The 60-source pilot panel and fixed November 11–25 UTC window remain unchanged. Segment observations at deployment. Zero paid-provider spend, shared runtime locking, metadata attribution, source/reference photos, local translation integrity and all earlier release cutovers are preserved.
