# Mission 6 — Varieties workspace

October 1, 2026. Review checkpoint on `feature/variety-workspace-consolidation`, stacked on Company Mission 5. Not merged or deployed.

## Purpose and destinations

**Find a variety → inspect its sources and roles → compare or investigate a location.** The default `/entities/variety` uses the approved bright Glasshouse shell and a compact alphabetical table. The review corpus has 64 catalog varieties; this remains the existing catalog, not a count of all available cultivars. Name/alias/trade-name/code search, berry, linked geography, organization relationship and existing rights/retail/performance/signal presence filters are retained. The organization chooser also reaches brands and breeding programs.

Directory company favorites, Tier 1/2/3/Untiered and lists read the same personal store as Companies, News, Map and Digest. These filters use active recorded breeder/owner/licensee/grower/trial/marketer/distributor relationships with organizations. Photo associations and people do not establish those roles. Unassigned organizational tiers stay Untiered; a variety without a recorded company role does not acquire a fictional Untiered company. Supplemental licensee/grower/trial/distributor roles are visible alongside existing breeder/owner/marketer presentations. Existing catalog relationship semantics are retained for the separate organization chooser.

A–Z is accent folded, with `#` for other initials. Letters retain current filters. Growing-location filtering reads the shared Mission 4 catalog and private annotations; selling markets, IP jurisdictions and unrelated company locations cannot create growing locations. Focus directory brings results into view without changing scope. Wide columns scroll inside the table, including on mobile.

Competition and Retail observations remain separate lenses within Varieties. They reuse the existing backbone queries: Competition requires one berry and supports its observed-market and rights-plus-observation scope; Retail observations supports berry scope and retains unnamed listings. The UI explicitly distinguishes those scopes from Directory-only company marks and alphabet filters. No fabricated comparison score or new data object is introduced. Compare and Coverage remain linked specialist tools; they retain their existing presentation pending their own migration.

## Profiles and review

Variety profiles retain identity notes, company roles, rights filings, commercial observations, performance/production statements, traits, signals/candidates, assessments/recommendations/questions, timeline, relationships and Facts. News/source cards remain exposed and open the shared `#v2ReaderOffcanvas`, with Article/Brief modes, personal reading controls and a separate publisher link. Missing article text remains explicit; profile browsing does not request acquisition. Secondary record explanations and specialist sections are collapsed by default. Section links expand the relevant disclosure; all disclosures work without JavaScript.

Growing regions are visible in the profile with location, activity, status, date, source and basis, plus the existing region editor and Map links. User annotations remain labeled unreviewed. Other linked geographies and educational growing-trait context are distinct sections. A profile's recorded rights do not establish either a growing location or an independently measured product trait.

Photo imports at `/variety-seeds` and authoring-only Identity review at `/varieties/candidates` form a subworkflow, not competing catalog destinations. The existing 143 photo candidates and 144 company associations remain provisional, with 32 unclear cells held separately. The isolated review universe also contains corpus-discovered candidates: its current 177 rows are not 177 reviewed varieties. The compact queue supports name/code/company search, berry, photo-company, identity status and A–Z; expanding a row retains its source, possible match, notes and every existing decision control. Fragment links reveal the requested candidate. “Separate candidate” describes a distinct proposed name, not a confirmed cultivar. Confirm same remains an explicit human decision linking to an existing variety; Mark distinct does not create a canonical entity or company role.

Previous directory/profile presentation remains at `?view=legacy`. Original published static templates remain unchanged. No backend/domain schema, CPVO, Variety footprint, commercial-observation, Trade or Weather services are rewritten. No canonical identities, roles, Facts or Evidence are created. No collection or paid research runs on GET.
