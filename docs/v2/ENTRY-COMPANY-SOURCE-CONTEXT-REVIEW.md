## Scope and behavior

The original register's checked first-name heading scope stays partial. The
later additive snapshot preserves the prior snapshot bytes, all eight source
IDs, name/page links and 398-context accounting. It adds only an explicit Origin
organization description and existing company identifiers. It does not approve
new aliases, roles, company identities, current rights, traits, regions or photos.
The original PDF and full-text reading aids remain private; body-free provenance
and review evidence are committed.

Entry-level company context overrides the source-wide fallback. Older single
program snapshots keep their existing behavior, including failed-source gaps.
An explicit empty entry list means no entry association. Malformed associations
and missing attribution fail validation. Reconciliation carries each entry's
organizations into candidate provenance, company source-name tables and scoped
coverage counts. Source/company filters are applied together: a candidate cannot
borrow a company association from another source reference to pass the selected
source filter. No canonical writer, rights assessment, extraction provider or
acquisition schedule is introduced.
