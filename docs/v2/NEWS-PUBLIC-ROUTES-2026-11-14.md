# Public publisher and Japanese official-notice routes

The November 13 independent panel review could not reach WSJ's business
section or Tochigi's agriculture landing page. Two additional bounded routes
complement their existing coverage without changing the frozen panel.

WSJ's current public Dow Jones business RSS provides publisher titles,
summaries, links and publication dates. The old feeds.a.dj.com business feed
responded successfully but stopped at January 24, 2025; it is not configured.
The current feeds.content.dowjones.io input reached November 14, 2026. A saved
normal-provider replay yielded 82 metadata leads and no eligible berry story.
That proves current metadata transport, not berry-event coverage, article-body
access or complete coverage of WSJ's desks. Paywall limits remain unchanged.

Tochigi's native pest-notice page exposes crop-bearing PDF links. One listing
request checks at most 300 anchors and retains at most 30 distinct links, with
strict HTTPS/host/path rules. Original Japanese titles and local announcement
context survive. Only an explicit full date in the same announcement supplies
a publication day. Page updates, filenames and partial month/day labels never
supply an inferred publication day. Duplicate archive links preserve a known
announcement day. A challenge, missing document structure, oversized response
or transport failure fails the route rather than claiming a successful empty
check. Native pest/plant-protection vocabulary qualifies explicit crop leads;
it neither assigns a crop nor approves a source or technical recommendation.

The saved Japanese input yielded eleven crop leads: two dated September 18
and October 16, and nine with unknown publication days. The normal 30-day
collection retained the October 16 control on November 14; undated leads
remain discovery observations and do not enter dated News automatically.
No dated prospective pilot event was established by this replay. PDF issue
dates reviewed separately are not verified first-public visibility. Acquisition
of linked PDFs remains the existing bounded, excerpt-only partial-body path.
No new translation model or English presentation is installed by this change.

Priority routes increase from 55 to 57. The existing twelve priority and six
regional checks per refresh, provider-credit settings and every human trust
gate remain unchanged. Japanese query wording gains native pest terms within
the existing bounded query catalog; this is a new configuration cutover.

Source-shaped tests cover native dates, unknown and invalid dates, overlapping
links, all four crops, nonberry notices, unverified links, challenge/transport
failures, original metadata, UNREVIEWED state and default News projection.
Saved public-input replays are engineering controls, not independent recall
or latency wins. No controlled intake or production change is part of this PR.

The coordinated release owner must carry forward the latest accepted app,
reports/private catalogs, downloads, deployment mounts and user drafts, pass
current CI and capacity/backup checks, then verify actual ordinary route
receipts. Canary transport does not prove scheduled collection. The original
60-source panel, failed cohorts and November 25 pilot end stay unchanged.
