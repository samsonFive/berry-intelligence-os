# Global News product ambiguity controls

Review of the first three live News pages after global source expansion found two unrelated records: a CryptoSlate BlackBerry tokenized stock listing and strawberry-pattern shelf paper. These are consumed engineering controls, not independent precision samples. Both source records were unreviewed; neither required deleting original metadata or changing trust.

The shared BlackBerry ambiguity screen now recognizes tokenized-stock listing terminology while preserving explicit agricultural technology coverage. The shared discovery and News relevance screen excludes household shelf-paper/liner headlines. Negative controls run through discovery qualification and current News projection, so retained historical records receive the same correction. Positive controls preserve strawberry shelf-life packaging, berry traceability and farm robotics. The change does not blacklist publishers or individual URLs, infer crop identity from generated translations, or promise complete spam detection.

Provider spend, collection cadence, publisher routes, reference photo attribution and the original prospective pilot window are unchanged. Broader multilingual shopping, consumer-gardening and SEO noise remain part of independently reviewed precision measurement; two repaired cases do not establish a population score.
