# News relevance and English presentation recalibration

The deployed first-page audit reproduced carrot yields, pineapple sizes, a tomato launch, BlackBerry automotive coverage, SNAP benefits, Amazon's climate accelerator and Costa Rica/Jacksonville cargo among the newest stories. Incidental cultivar/company names and a requested-sustainability bypass admitted unrelated sources. Collection breadth is useful discovery evidence, but it does not establish a berry subject for the default News experience.

Default News now requires berry wording in the actual title/summary (including the regional vocabulary), or a sufficiently distinctive, berry-linked company as the headline subject. Inferred crop/entity assignments alone do not satisfy this gate. Broad research institutions and incidental company mentions cannot substitute for the crop subject. The existing metadata qualification still applies. Adjacent topic/sustainability coverage requires the explicit adjacent selection. Raw discoveries and archived records remain available; no publication, relationship or trust decision is changed. New non-berry produce exclusions and the BlackBerry CEO/automotive disambiguation also protect future qualification.

English language identification now covers Latin-script reporting using the existing offline language model. Scheduled collection uses only preinstalled CPU translation models for public titles/summaries; paid translation is blocked by the existing credit-free guard. Eight initial model languages are Portuguese, Spanish, French, Polish, Chinese, Japanese, Arabic and German. Release-time provisioning records model metadata, distribution URLs, archive SHA256 and extracted-file SHA256. Runtime performs no model downloads. Unsupported, uncertain and failed translations remain pending, never represented as translated. Cached English presentation is applied on reads without inference, requests, canonical prose writes or trust changes. Original metadata and URLs are preserved. Machine translation quality remains a review concern, especially cultivar names, figures and legal wording; article bodies are not translated by this feature.

News, saved stories and Readers use real publisher images where available. Missing or failed photos show a compact publisher placeholder, replacing the four repeated generated crop illustrations. No photograph is invented for an article.

Validation includes the reported production metadata regressions, incidental entity/crop assignment negatives, direct reporting across all four crops, mixed-crop and non-English positives, cached translation identity preservation, a regional local-only translation case, credit-free paid-call exclusion, and existing News/pilot/image workflows. Release acceptance additionally requires isolated real-model inference with networking disabled and production first-page checks.
