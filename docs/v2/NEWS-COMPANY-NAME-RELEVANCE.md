# Keep software-company stories out of berry News

The live News feed showed “BlackBerry’s Phil Kurtz on sovereignty, licensing
and the long game” as blackberry news. A company-name collision made a software
licensing headline look like berry-industry coverage. Existing discovery rules
caught some device/stock stories, while cached Today metadata had no equivalent
company check. Stored News also reuses the acquisition qualifier.

A shared context check now identifies BlackBerry company coverage from specific
software/device cues, or the distinctive brand spelling together with corporate
language. A name or the word “licensing” alone is insufficient. Explicit fruit,
cultivar, breeding, grower, harvest or agricultural context keeps legitimate
berry and mixed agricultural technology stories available. Fresh qualification,
Today filtering and stored News selection use the same distinction. Existing
operator-selected sustainability-feed exceptions remain.

This is a metadata selection change. It does not fetch articles, delete or
rewrite captured records, change saved feedback, approve source statements or
create trusted material. Historical captures remain available through their
existing source/Reader paths. News still preserves its publication order,
scope, article IDs, images and Reader actions. No new Source, company identity,
berry assignment, provider, model call or content-coverage claim is added.

The native phone review also found an existing overflow: adding the workspace
save button squeezed the Reader action row beyond its panel. The shared toolbar
and Reader header now wrap their controls instead of shrinking or clipping
them. Desktop layouts retain a single row when space permits. No action is
hidden or removed; this small layout repair is verified in the real browser.

The regression set includes the observed headline, device/software/corporate
variants, fruit licensing and patent controls, and mixed orchard technology.
A real stale-cache read verifies filtered results and counts without refetching
or changing any stored cache/tag bytes. News selection verifies original image
links, record IDs, retained source text and human feedback. The first affected
run passed 108 checks with one existing warning in 25.80 seconds. Native review
and all four exact-head CI checks are required before this separate draft is
ready; their final receipts belong in the PR and durable mission ledger.

TD-015 stays open for the separate legacy relevance-screen/acquisition lane.
This change fixes the demonstrated current News case; it does not establish
perfect relevance across every language, publisher or ambiguous company name.
The canonical expansion guide and all 31 accepted requirement definitions and
their ordering remain unchanged. This draft is independent of the frozen
platform/catalog release candidates; software release approval remains separate
from source, identity, catalog and image-permission decisions.
