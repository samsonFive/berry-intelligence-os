# PSG catalog recovery

The previously unreadable Plant Sciences Genetics selectors now yield **27
source cards for the United States**: 19 strawberry, seven raspberry and one
blackberry. Two detail-page related lists supply another 17 occurrences. The
44 occurrences describe 34 distinct printed names or codes; 18 are additional
review candidates, bringing the combined public-source candidate set from
1,315 to 1,333 while retaining every earlier key.

This is a source-data proposal on deployed platform #486 (`b8b8b008`), separate
from News reliability #487 and documentation #485. It does not add approved
varieties or change application behavior. CI, desktop/phone review and a fresh
isolated live-data preservation check are release gates. No release approval
has been given for this proposal.

## What was checked

The publisher's [strawberry selector](https://www.plantsciencesgenetics.com/variety-selection?cropName=Strawberry)
and its Raspberry and Blackberry views were allowed to finish rendering, with
United States of America, all regions and all types selected. Each of the 27
cards was opened and its printed full code checked on the actual detail page.
The related cards beside [Inspire](https://www.plantsciencesgenetics.com/variety-details?varietyId=B07CDB23-68B3-86B5-AEDF-3C08A4AB94E1&cropName=Strawberry)
and [Resolution](https://www.plantsciencesgenetics.com/variety-details?varietyId=B0CEDFAB-9C65-CC59-7CEA-DDEEA6F1DAE0&cropName=Strawberry)
were also recorded. Retrieval was November 3; no publication date was inferred.

The dated import retains literal headings, full codes, observed country/filter
scope, actual detail destinations and publisher patent/datasheet references.
Related lists do not inherit the US selector scope. Country suitability is not
proof of actual planting, availability, licensed territory or market share.
Company publication is not proof of breeder, owner or licensee roles.

The short heading **060** appears with both `VS-17.5417-060` and
`SB_15_089-060`. They remain separate full-code candidates. Numeric headings use
their full printed identifier as the candidate name; named products retain
their names. Display labels and codes stay separate. No official denomination,
trade name or alias is inferred. `BK6-13` is not an additional cultivar and
Rejoice is not imported as one.

All 34 selected photos were visually checked beside their source labels/codes.
They remain attributed URL references with unknown reuse permission and
unconfirmed independent botanical identity. They never load automatically or
enter public output; the existing explicit private-session preview remains.
No publisher image is downloaded or republished by this import.

Publisher patent numbers do not establish current legal status or claim scope.
The existing [original USPP34597P2 record](https://patents.google.com/patent/USPP34597P2/en)
and its claim/figure links remain; no duplicate patent source is created.
Linked datasheet bodies were not newly checked in this capture.
