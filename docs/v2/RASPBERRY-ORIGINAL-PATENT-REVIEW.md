# Raspberry patent originals and attributed fruit references

The source lists already contained several ABB codes, while their original
filings were missing from those entries. This follow-up adds four grant-document
references, direct links to the inspected claim pages and four labeled fruit
figures. It is an additional research draft stacked on catalog #400; it does not
alter the platform release or approve identities.

All claim links point to PDF page 3. Title, description/caption, claim and fruit
pages were inspected from the original scans. PDF page count and SHA-256 are
recorded per filing; no complete visual review of every drawing page is claimed.
The four PNG references were downloaded and checked against the labeled figures.
They remain hidden by default, with attribution and the existing private
session-only choice. Patent hosting supplies no photo reuse permission.

The originals name Allberry B.V. as applicant/assignee and Niels Arts as inventor.
Those historical document roles do not approve a relationship or equivalence
with Advanced Berry Breeding. The original claims use their literal ABB codes.
They do not themselves establish that a commercial name is an approved alias.

The [issuer's January 2024 launch](https://www.abbreeding.nl/2024/01/24/abb-announces-commercial-launch-three-new-raspberry-varieties/)
separately pairs Malaika with ABB135, Zawadi with ABB136 and Baridi with ABB132.
Its Baridi code differs from the already retained [2025 announcement](https://www.abbreeding.nl/2025/01/20/discover-the-new-standard-of-abbs-raspberries-at-fruit-logistica-2025/),
which prints ABB 137. This batch adds evidence for review, without selecting an
alias or transferring a patent/photo onto Baridi, Malaika or Zawadi.

Twelve parent/comparison occurrences are separated from the four focal claims.
The ABB 135 text uses both 211150-009 and 211150-9 for its pollen parent; both
literal spellings remain visible. Numeric selections and parent/comparator names
inherit no focal grant, figures, applicant role, traits or growing regions.
References to earlier patents/reissues remain leads requiring their own original
document checks, not automatically verified records.

CPVO applications 20221155, 20221158, 20221159 and 20221156 appear as priority
claims dated 2022-05-04. They are not entered as European grant numbers or
current legal status. Google's status/expiry assumptions are not copied.

This adds nine unreviewed source sections and nineteen scoped name occurrences,
not nineteen new cultivars. The 77-subject plan, independent human worksheet,
canonical catalog and all 31 accepted requirements remain. Full current and
historical portfolio coverage, original-source access, current rights, reviewed
identities and catalog authoring remain open under CAT-01/CAT-02 and TD-116.
