# Four-berry cultivar register acquisition

The original 2024 *Register of New Fruit and Nut Cultivars List 52* is available
from a [New Mexico State University copy](https://jujube.nmsu.edu/documents/2024-fruit-cultivar-registration-52-hortsci-article-p1220.pdf).
It is a broad source for the next portfolio audit across all four berry crops.
It has not been imported into the catalog or treated as a complete global list.

The publisher PDF request returned non-PDF content, the web reader returned 403,
and the native browser displayed an unreadable gray PDF. The university copy was
retrieved without bypassing access controls. The original title, journal issue,
printed pages and online-publication line were checked against the document,
rather than inferred from the university filename. The copy has 73 PDF pages;
its SHA-256 and reading scope are retained in the acquisition artifact.

The automated reading aid is not approved for import.

## A concrete identity issue

The raspberry entry on PDF page 50 describes the Hutton selection *Bonnie Lewis*,
also named *Lewis*, and separately points out USDA's earlier cultivar of that name.
This supports keeping the two leads unresolved rather than merging them on their
shared label. It adds an original-source reference for a human identity review;
it does not submit that decision or change any catalog record. Its historical
rights references require separate current official verification.

## Next program step

1. Account for every described cultivar and every referral in the four crop
   sections, then audit the four-berry addenda. Preserve printed spellings,
   denominations and source page links; hold unreadable glyphs and uncertain pairs.
2. Compare that bounded inventory against current observations and catalog
   records. Keep historical names, code/name conflicts and genuinely new leads
   distinguishable. Verify prior candidate IDs and operator decisions on replay.
3. Link original filings where identified, and separately verify cited profile
   claims, role assignments, current rights, regions and photo reuse. The register
   does not approve any of those fields by itself.
4. Extend the historical audit using the Pomological Society's earlier register
   archive after this current register has been accounted. Do not call this source
   exhaustive or use automated matching as the independent human recall benchmark.

Original PDF/text/rendered reading aids stay in the private ignored acquisition
folder. Only body-free provenance is committed. No source acceptance, extraction
