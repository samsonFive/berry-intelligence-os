# Mission 23 — Source-assisted region entries and profile recovery

Region editing now starts from a linked article or reviewed statement. The source's literal summary or statement leads a readable preview, with publisher, publication date, review state, the shared article Reader and original source link. Its source reference, URL and exact publication date carry into a new unsaved entry. A person still chooses the location, activity and effective date. Known place mentions are reading context, never an inferred operating/growing edge. No news co-mention, office, patent territory or selling market becomes a growing location automatically.

The same flow serves company Operating regions and variety Growing regions at `/profiles/{id}/regions`. Statement support can supply its actual Evidence reference even when the article's own entity list is incomplete. Only active statements linked to the profile are eligible. Missing or timestamp-only publication dates remain empty; unsafe source URLs are not revived. A prepared entry defaults to Proposed. Preparing never writes an annotation or calls a provider. It cannot replace a current edit. Save uses the existing private annotation, revision, history and Map/profile projection, preserving canonical relationships and human review gates.

Company Profile & links and People now offer readable history with recovery. Restore the three link fields or one contact from a saved version, preserving other contacts, unrelated links, favorites, tier, lists, watches and canonical records. Link absence still means collected fallback; an intentional blank remains blank. A restore requires the current revision and a snapshot belonging to that company, rejects unsafe historical URLs, and appends a new before/after history entry. Old snapshots remain intact. History starts closed and does not appear in the read-only public profile.
