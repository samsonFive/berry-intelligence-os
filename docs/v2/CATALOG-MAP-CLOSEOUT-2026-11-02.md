# Catalog and Map closeout review

This draft brings together the prepared catalog/Map candidate #449 and the
original-source follow-ups through #467 on current canonical/live #470
(`d2f6eb96`). It retains the private patent research and original-row spreadsheet
that were deployed separately. Frozen component heads and their earlier review
questions remain historical evidence; their approvals are not inherited here.
Platform #468 and Learn #464 remain separate release scopes.

## What the analyst gains

Original interviews, product sheets and Extension tables now reach the same
variety-name review workflow. The combined public seed has **537 source sections
and 2,029 literal name occurrences**: 625 blueberry, 786 strawberry, 403 raspberry
and 215 blackberry. Repeated names are retained with their individual sources;
these are not 2,029 distinct varieties. There are 175 catalog text matches and
1,854 occurrences still needing identity review. The combined candidate view
contains 1,315 review keys, retaining earlier source keys and saved decisions.

Of the original 77 supplied subjects, 67 have named-source entries, seven remain
partial, one unavailable and two held for identity clarification. Even a subject
with names can have an incomplete portfolio. All 146 follow-up sections and 14
unreadable sections remain visible. Historical interviews do not become current
product inventories; publisher associations do not establish breeder, owner,
licensee or marketer roles.

The manual Map workflow adds separately identified Eurostat berry series and
the official UK fruit workbook. Retained captures offer **24 country/crop groups
and 51 figures across eight countries**. Each figure retains source, year, unit,
precision and provisional/withholding notes. The two unresolved UK area
comparisons remain visible. No annual yield is inferred and no reference is
selected automatically. Existing selected figures, history and legacy jobs
must remain intact.

Credited variety photo references remain subject to existing association and
reuse checks. Unknown permission is held; an explicit private-session preview
does not grant hosting rights or make the image load by default. Named original
patent/rights links retain document and legal-stage context without asserting
that rights are currently active.

## Preservation and verification

All six versioned import files from #449 and fourteen from #467 are additions;
their original bytes are retained. Existing canonical identities, Evidence,
Facts, relationships, schemas and the published market-reference seed are not
rewritten. The eight application/script files for the Map workflow retain #449's
tested implementation. The canonical private-research and spreadsheet routes
remain unchanged. The expansion guide and the original 31 accepted requirement
definitions/order remain verbatim.

The joined audit, whole-earlier-key preservation, source metadata comparisons
and existing component regressions must pass on this exact combined tree.
The local combination proof retains all 2,974 existing canonical data files,
both components' earlier review keys, and 12 edited/rejected candidate records
across all four berries, with identical replay. The initial broad check passed
376 cases and exposed four Windows test-decoding failures; explicit UTF-8
reads repair those without changing source bytes or assertions. All 106 cases
in the repaired-file and Map navigation/export check pass.
Final full CI, record/static leakage validation, native desktop/phone/PDF review
and a fresh bounded live-data preservation check are release gates. Component
receipts alone do not prove the combined release safe. New import collisions
must preserve user-edited existing files rather than replace them.

## Work that remains human or ongoing

Source-copy acceptance, literal-name review, company roles, canonical authoring,
current-rights decisions and photo permission remain independent gates. The 32
unclear photographed seed cells, two organization labels and independent
32-source expected-name worksheet are retained in the closeout packet. Opening
a source, exporting or passing tests cannot answer these decisions.

This is measurable coverage improvement, not proof of the most comprehensive
resource on the web. Worldwide current/historical recall, traits, supported
growing regions and independent name-coverage review remain open under TD-116.
Cold loading remains a TD-122 limitation; this source/Map release does not claim
the separate performance work is deployed.

After exact-head verification, this combined scope needs its own release
