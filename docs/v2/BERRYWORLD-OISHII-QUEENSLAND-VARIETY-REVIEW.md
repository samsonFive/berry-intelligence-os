## Source scope

- [BerryWorld South Africa](https://www.berryworld.com/en-za/berries/varieties):
  the current non-exhaustive list names seven strawberries, ten raspberries,
  two blackberries and nineteen blueberries. Four additional literal labels
  from the awards history stay historical. Award years are not page publication
  dates. The footer distinguishes BerryWorld SA from the North American brand
  operated by Mastronardi Produce. No automatic AgroBerries attribution follows
  from a corporate transaction or the registry's combined row.
- BerryWorld prefixes are retained. BerryWorld Gem, BerryGem and Diamond Jubilee
  remain literal source labels. The historical paired Gem/Jubilee award line is
  a naming lead, not an approved alias. Masena/BerryWorld Masena and Eureka
  Dawn/BerryWorld Eureka Dawn likewise remain unresolved until identity review.
  Existing approved catalog aliases can still match; this batch approves none.
  Breeding-programme headings are source context, not approved company roles or
  exclusive rights. Generic crop cutouts and mixed-berry pictures are withheld.
- Oishii's [Koyo](https://oishii.com/pages/the-koyo-berry),
  [Omakase](https://oishii.com/pages/the-omakase-berry) and
  [Nikko](https://oishii.com/pages/the-nikko-berry) pages retain their full marketed
  headings as proposed labels. These are not asserted official denominations or
  breeder codes. The [expanded FAQ](https://oishii.com/pages/faq) describes its
  berries as strawberry varietals, supplementing Nikko's product-page crop
  context. Tomato, product grade and preserves are excluded from strawberry
  identities. Source claims about flavor, yield or farms remain unapproved.
- [Queensland's archived 2018 report abstract](https://era.dpi.qld.gov.au/id/eprint/6539/)
  lists twelve strawberries. The seven-name subtropical group is described as
  six; the discrepancy stays visible through accounting and a follow-up. The
  other groups list two and three. Citation year, archive date and modification
  date stay distinct; no exact publication day is invented. Fanfare- ASBP's
  literal spacing is preserved. The full linked report and rights filings have
  not been checked in this capture. Climate target groups do not establish
  actual growing regions, and breeding targets are not confirmed traits.
