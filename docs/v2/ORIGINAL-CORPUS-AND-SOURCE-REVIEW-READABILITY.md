## Review readability

The authenticity reader previously projected only passage text and index,
dropping the acquisition's publisher heading hints. It now reuses the shared
article-block validator: only stored, unique, valid heading hints become
headings. Duplicate text and invalid hints remain literal prose. It preserves
every passage and index and escapes source HTML. Nothing is rewritten or generated.

Primary source sections have a 20px heading with a divider; ordinary text is
16px on desktop and 15px on phone. Lower source headings have a distinct 17px
style. Paragraph references remain in `data-source-paragraph` and hover metadata.
Transcript timestamps and speakers retain their existing rendering. Provenance,
matching details and consequences stay secondary; source differences and the
separate confirmation checkbox remain visible and unchanged.

Native review used the real pending Arkansas copy: its four retained headings
appear in the review page. Phone client/scroll width is **375/375 px**, section
heading **20px**, body **15px**. No real approval, rejection or identity decision
was performed. Only the verified isolated localhost preview process was restarted
to load the updated projection; its data and inbox were preserved.
