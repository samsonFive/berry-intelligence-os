# Variety source parity: audit and first repair

User acceptance standard: a company website or ordinary public search should not routinely reveal material variety information absent from this workspace. The target is a maintained, cited variety dossier, not just name discovery or news mentions. This checkpoint does not claim that standard is met globally. Deployment and merge remain on hold.

## Current evidence

The checked-out canonical snapshot contains 64 varieties (41 blueberry, 12 raspberry, six strawberry, five blackberry), 1,272 published source records, 186 Facts, 255 relationships and 395 dated portfolio source sections. Thirty-three varieties have no structured trait entries. Among the 1,272 source records, the source-body classifier finds 1,199 without readable article text and 73 with access-screen content; none contains a readable article body. This is a statement about this checkout's stored published records, not the production database, private Reader recoveries or newly collected pending articles.

The search-review preview was materially under-provisioned for variety review: it had the 64 identity records but no published source records, Facts, relationships or portfolio sections. Existing canonical public records and the relevant observation imports have now been copied into the isolated preview without overwriting existing preview records. Its pending articles, keyword settings and account state remain. This is a test-data restoration, not publication of new Evidence or a production-data modification.

## Source comparisons

- **Ervin:** the catalog has its NC 740 alias and one structured flavor entry. The [NC State release](https://rubus.ces.ncsu.edu/news/meet-ervin-blackberry/) also discusses fruiting habit, thornlessness, harvest timing, fruit quality/postharvest, regional adaptation and licensing contacts. [GPG's introduction](https://www.globalplantgenetics.com/introducing-ervin-the-latest-introduction-nc-states-breeding-programme/) adds international licensing/development context. Three previously captured pending Ervin articles were not linked to the variety by `entity_ids`, so the profile could remain sparse despite readable local sources. The first repair displays these as unreviewed exact-name source mentions, not approved variety links or reviewed traits.
- **Eureka:** existing structured information is richer and already preserves a patent/marketing firmness distinction and cultivar/brand ambiguity. Its source references previously lacked retained text. Two existing primary URLs, Mountain Blue and BerryWorld, now have private Reader captures in the preview (one full, one partial). A reference to the Eureka family is not proof that every statement applies to Eureka/Ridley 1403. Eureka Sunrise and other longer identities must not be attached to Eureka through a contained-name match.
- **Ventura:** present in four portfolio observations, but absent from the 64 canonical profiles. [Fall Creek's product page](https://www.fallcreeknursery.com/en/commercial-fruit-growers/varieties/ventura) supplies a useful field set: climate/chill, management, season, size, firmness, habit, use and licensing context. Its page also includes a testimonial about LunaBlue; blindly copying every nearby claim would attribute another variety's text to Ventura. Existing source-name/identity review must precede canonical expansion.
- **G-Viva, Pola and Soten:** native article captures now exist, but the canonical catalog lacks these varieties. The finite stored portfolio observations inspected do not include them. The existing general article-name parser also returned no leads in a diagnostic over these pending source texts. This is a remaining explicit-name extraction/intake gap, not permission to invent identities or promote their claims.

## First repair in this candidate

A private **Source details & profile gaps** section now appears on existing variety profiles. It reads selected stored source text and offers the existing in-app Reader, original source and publication-review links. It exposes recorded source dates, capture timestamps, missing text and whether a source association is only an unreviewed name mention. It does not summarize sources into newly approved trait values.

Selection reuses exact catalog-name/alias matching and identity resolution, rejects wrong-crop and ambiguous matches, suppresses contained names such as Eureka inside Eureka Sunrise, and honors already recorded evidence/entity links. Metadata selection precedes a maximum of twelve source hydrations; excerpts and expanded passages are bounded. Existing stored article text and reviewed recoveries win over a Reader cache. Reader captures must match the record ID and exact requested URL, pass availability checks and remain bounded. New captured text remains visibly unreviewed even when the older source record was published.

No route performs acquisition or identity writes while displaying a profile. The section is excluded from read-only/public and static output and performs no Fact, trait, rights, relationship or footprint writes. Rejected/archived and Atomic records do not enter it. No Variety backend/domain schema, CPVO, growing-footprint or commercial-observation model was changed.

## Remaining work in the existing variety mission

Continue `VARIETY-CATALOG-COMPREHENSIVENESS-MISSION.md`, rather than starting a parallel catalog:

1. Enumerate complete breeder/university/company portfolios across the four berries, accounting for each name, code and exclusion. Capture product pages, technical sheets, trial reports, release notices and rights references; a news feed or catalog-name count is insufficient.
2. Maintain field coverage for identity/parentage, breeder and separate commercial roles, fruiting/chill/season, fruit size/quality/flavor, yield and trial conditions, postharvest, disease claims, production systems, licensing/territory, attributed photos and current source checks. Unknown fields stay unknown; marketing and independent measurements stay distinct.
3. Fix demonstrated explicit native-name intake gaps using real held-out source examples. Preserve human identity decisions and coded-name collisions. Discovering a name does not automatically approve a catalog identity, alias or company role.
4. Use existing publication and claim review to reconcile source assertions into structured profiles. Show conflicting statements and their conditions rather than overwriting them. Registration territory and recommended climate do not establish growing footprint.
5. Measure source-to-profile parity on a named, independently checked sample per berry: fields present on primary pages, captured/readable fields, visible source passages, reviewed structured fields, omissions, wrong-variety attribution and freshness. This audit is an initial diagnostic, not that acceptance score.

Raw inventory and capture receipts stay in `inbox/search-quality-evaluation/variety-source-parity/`; private bodies and screenshots do not belong in the public PR. The first UI repair makes captured information visible and gaps actionable, while comprehensive acquisition, structured enrichment and independent recall remain open.
