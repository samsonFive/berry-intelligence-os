   matrix. Open a genetics portfolio and trace its sources. Offices, trials,
   plans and IP enforcement remain distinguishable from production. Close
   evidence to recover the full comparison width.
2. **Are missing varieties visible?** Open a named source or search its company.
   Names missing from the catalog appear as review items. A name or patent
   reference does not automatically approve an alias, breeder, right or growing
   region. Use the existing review and catalog-authoring handoffs separately.
3. **Can you work through the catalog efficiently?** Use the candidate list's
   persistent alphabet navigation. In profile coverage, filter the missing
   field, then pan the phone table: variety names stay pinned, company names
   wrap fully and headers remain visible further down the rows. Open a profile
   for attributed photos, original patent/PVR references and cited traits.
   Unknown photo reuse stays withheld by default; session preview does not
   create permission or publish an image.
4. **Does the guide explain the app?** Its eleven section cards explain purpose;
   four analyst steps show the workflow. Six output cards distinguish Meeting
   Prep, a saved brief, an editable report/PDF, a market snapshot, a competitor
   JSON packet and a blueberry Landscape briefing. Live workspaces, refreshed
   selectors and frozen downloads have different behavior.
5. **Is the briefing useful?** Inspect the Landscape HTML/SVG/CSV output and
   its sources, dates, complete selection and qualifications. Other berries,
