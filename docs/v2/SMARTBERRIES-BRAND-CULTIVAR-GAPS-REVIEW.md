# Smart Berries: original brands and missing cultivar inventory

The company’s original pages describe different blueberry and raspberry varieties
and exclusive blackberry selections, without identifying their cultivar names.
The app now exposes this gap and the exact sections checked. **Betty Blues** and
**Ruby Reds** are explicitly wholesale brands; they are not new variety entries.
