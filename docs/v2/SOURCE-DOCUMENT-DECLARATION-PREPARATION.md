# Avoid unused preparation for source documents

The private variety audit walks every paragraph of a stored article even when
the article has none of the declaration syntax supported by its parsers. This
change returns the existing empty result before that unused work. Articles
that may contain a declaration retain every original parser, crop scope,
exclusion, source label and identity/review rule.

One runtime file changes. This adds no cache, stored projection, acquisition,
provider request, new grammar, source filter or background warmup. It does not
change the source inventory or turn candidates into canonical varieties.

## Correctness boundary

The gate covers the necessary prefixes of every current base and article
format: singular/plural variety and cultivar wording, denomination and selection
declarations, the retained Spanish/Polish forms, and pipe tables. Positive
documents run through their complete original paragraph sequence, retaining
profile-heading distance and contextual exclusions. An unknown crop remains
unknown; publication tags do not become article evidence.

Source title validation still runs first. Unsupported metadata and dotted or
dotless-I crop cases keep their original path, including existing errors. The
576-case behavior comparison retains all complete results and 256 matching
legacy errors. The implemented change passes 284 focused grammar, discovery,
source-handoff and human-review checks with zero attempted external connections.
All four berry crops are explicitly covered.

The necessary-syntax gate must be extended when a future supported declaration
introduces new syntax. It is not a completeness test or a new extractor.

## Measured prototype and final acceptance

The bounded prototype compares eight fresh processes, two per variant/order.
Each process measures an initial Company or Candidates page, the other page,
then a new fictional unreviewed article arriving in memory before another visit.
The fictional story never writes production or fixture source files. Its raw
draft remains in review and its read projection is explicitly Unreviewed.

Complete typed article bundles, the full unfiltered universe, complete typed
response contexts and HTML match for all 24 responses. Source-copy bytes and
healthy live state remain unchanged. The prototype avoids unused preparation
in 2,874 of 3,115 source-parser calls across the two calculations; these are
call counts, not counts of approved varieties or sources removed.

These figures are prototype measurements on one tested copy, not live or
global performance claims. The exact implemented module has its own complete
workflow comparison. Release acceptance also requires current-head CI, native
desktop/phone review and fresh production-copy preservation, recorded in the
PR and private release receipts. Two samples per variant are limited evidence.
Cold visits still take several seconds; TD-122 remains open.

The earlier Reader-key, whole-article memo and per-article parser memo experiments
are excluded after mixed/regressing whole-workflow results. Passing correctness
checks alone did not make them release candidates. Restart-model reuse remains
a separate diagnostic, not an implemented persistent cache.

The original 31 requirement definitions/order, governing guide, protected
schemas, stored records and human source/name/role/rights/photo/Map decisions
remain unchanged. The visual Help explainer, collaborative Workspaces and
private Digest/layout boundaries remain delivered. This change does not close
the outstanding human recall audit or claim worldwide catalog completeness.
