# November 12 News coverage and presentation remedies

The independently frozen November 12 cohort found a Moroccan berry logistics
qualification miss and a German blackberry export discovery miss. This change
addresses their mechanisms and three source-reviewed presentation defects.
Recovery remains an engineering control; it does not rescore the frozen pilot.

## Discovery and relevance

The shared material-news vocabulary now recognizes berry shipments, logistics
and transport. Specific non-target crop exclusions still apply, including
cranberry-only stories. Mixed reports with an explicit target berry remain
eligible. German Exportposition wording survives both relevance screens.

A Fruchthandel priority route reads one public homepage and at most four
crop-headline article metadata pages: five requests, eight seconds per request,
two MB per response, no redirects, pagination, off-host links or access bypass.
It retains native headlines, source URLs and only a matching main article's
timezone-bearing publication time. Sidebar dates are excluded. Publication
time remains distinct from first-public visibility; unknown dates stay unknown.
Failed article metadata requests fail the route rather than report complete
success. The configured priority count becomes 55 on deployment; refresh
budgets remain 12 priority and six regional routes.

The frozen independent listing snapshot verified a public /news request
resolving to the homepage and exposing the blackberry headline. The frozen
article HTML verified its article-local timestamp. Offline source-shaped
regressions exercise this path. Deployment, ordinary scheduled attempts,
production retention and fresh current visibility remain release-owner checks.
Successful transport alone will not establish event coverage.

## Source-preserving presentation

The reviewed Polish breeding headline replaces the observed literal idiom
“rearing from the back” with “the breeding process behind the scenes”. This
repair is restricted to that exact source headline and observed phrase. The
remaining text and numeric/currency checks stay intact; caches and originals
are not rewritten. This is not a general semantic-accuracy guarantee.

Normal Reader capture selects a source-exact paragraph mentioning a crop in
the headline. A bounded metadata preview can use it when the unreviewed,
untranslated original summary contains none of those crops. The Belgian VBT
report can therefore show its strawberry price paragraph instead of leading
with tomatoes. Published prose, translations, changed source identities and
already relevant summaries are protected. Originals and full captured passages
are retained. Browse reads only the small preview, without body loads, network
requests or models. Image refresh preserves a matching preview excerpt.

The exact gob.pe /assets/escudo-rojo- crest family is excluded from article
photo presentation. The existing credited berry reference catalog supplies the
fallback. Actual government article photographs remain eligible. This adds no
generated assets, provider cost or rights assertions.

Existing Reader captures need a normal preview regeneration before the crop
excerpt can appear; code deployment alone does not backfill them. The release
owner can load the retained VBT capture, verify its source identity and call
save_capture under the normal runtime lock. No new acquisition or translation
is required. Verify the current default News card afterward and retain the
before/after evidence. Do not rewrite the original summary.
