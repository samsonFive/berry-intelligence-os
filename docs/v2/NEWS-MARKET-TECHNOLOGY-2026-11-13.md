# Market reports and crop-production technology in News

The November 13 source review found two relevant reports missing from News:
BelOrta's autumn strawberry/blackberry market report and Bogaerts' Bull-Qii DS
platform for strawberry UV-C treatment. The former had a raw observation
without a qualified-stage observation or retained original. The latter had
raw and qualified observations and a retained original, but the current News
metadata screen excluded it.

Both reports expose a vocabulary gap. BelOrta's feed preview ends before
the later price/production details; its headline says “market position”.
Bogaerts' preview names strawberry crops, greenhouses, autonomous navigation
and UV-C, without the narrower production/genetics words recognized before.
Known publisher context could carry a record through collection while the
provider-neutral News screen still rejected it.

Shared material-news recognition now accepts commercial market wording and
requires production-setting context for robotics, automation or UV-C. These
checks apply to all four crops and unknown publishers; there are no URL or
publisher exemptions. Current source metadata remains the relevance input.
No body fetch, translation, new query, model call or acquisition is added to
browsing. Route counts and refresh budgets remain unchanged.

Precision controls remain active. Currant-only collective-berry market copy
is excluded alongside cranberry-only and unrelated produce. Mixed reports
with an explicit target crop remain eligible. The shared picking rule keeps
consumer visitor outings out while allowing actual greenhouse picking robots;
generic commercial picking and a robot's weekend launch date do not imply an outing.
collection and News use the same distinction. Stock/device, decorative
product, recipe and home-gardening exclusions remain in place.

Regression checks cover both source-shaped reports through qualification,
normal collection retention, unreviewed record construction and default News
projection. They preserve the source URL/title/summary and trust state.
Controls span all four crops, mixed strawberry/redcurrant reporting,
non-target crops, BlackBerry automotive stock, shelf paper, recipes, home
gardens, picking visitors and production picking robots. Saved public RSS
inputs also replay through the normal specialist provider and collection
services without network calls or production writes.

The coordinated release owner must reconcile the latest accepted report,
client, image and Compose state, complete all five CI gates and satisfy the
existing capacity/backup checks before deploying. An ordinary collection
refresh should retain BelOrta while the retained Bogaerts original should
become visible through the corrected projection. Verify both exact publisher
links in a fresh News projection. If a feed no longer exposes the known
BelOrta URL, any normal source-backed intake is a separate controlled recovery,
never a reason to bypass deduplication or publication review.

Preserve the November 13 frozen misses and all earlier results. Recoveries
