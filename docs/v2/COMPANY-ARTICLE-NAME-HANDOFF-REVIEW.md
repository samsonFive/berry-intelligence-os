# Names in captured news reach company variety review

The retained July 30 Hortifrut article names Vicentina as a raspberry variety.
It is readable in the shared Reader, but the private scan returns no name.
Company Varieties also displays only portfolio observations, discarding
article-derived candidates from the same already-built universe. These are two
reproducible CAT-01 gaps; neither permits an automatic canonical entity or role.

The narrow article rule recognizes an explicit possessive name immediately
followed by a supported crop and the singular word variety/cultivar. The adjacent
crop supplies scope even in a multi-crop story. The owner phrase remains context,
never a breeder, rights, award, trait or growing-location decision. Existing
quotation/list/profile/title rules remain. Family/program/flavor prose, missing
crop declarations and negated/uncertain prefixes do not qualify.

The company projection reuses current mentions, candidate decisions and body-free
metadata from the already-selected exact-source article inventory. It makes no
second corpus scan, matching decision, capture or provider call. Each article
keeps its own company co-mentions, URL, publication date and source-review state.
Publication review does not approve a variety identity or company relationship.

Private Company Varieties and its detailed portfolio retain their original rows.
Additional entries say **Named in captured news** and have candidate/catalog,
publisher and **Read captured article** links. They share existing search, crop
and alphabet controls. Overlapping portfolio/news names are not silently merged
into an approved identity/code. Secondary context stays collapsed.

Human rejection remains closed. A distinct-identity decision says Catalog entry
pending. Human matched identities link directly only when the target still
exists; otherwise the handoff stays in candidate review. Existing reviewed
company links remain above. Article context never enters read-only/static output.
GETs persist no candidates and change no sources, notes, decisions or user edits.
