# Mission 2 — Competitor news packets and operator variety seeds

September 30, 2026. Review checkpoint stacked on Mission 1; not merged or deployed.

## Purpose and delivered workflow

`/news-packets` creates a portable JSON news packet for a selected custom list. Choose an inclusive date range and **Reviewed only** or **All news**, run fresh capture, inspect the validated preview and exceptions, then generate/download. Every new generation requires a successful capture completed within 15 minutes; failed searches or validation block export. Existing downloads preserve the original packet and timestamp. A per-list **Last generated** date updates when a packet is created, never moves backwards on retry, and can prefill the next date range. Dates are inclusive, so a range starting on the last export day intentionally overlaps; stable IDs support receiving-side upserts.

Reviewed means the source publication passed its existing publication gate, not that individual statements were verified. Unapproved automatic or live capture cannot assert review through a label. Capture always runs in either mode; Reviewed only can return no newly discovered stories until publication review. Reader feedback, exports and lists never approve sources or claims. `publish_to_landscape` stays false for every exported row.

The first **Competitor registry** list reconciles the photo's 77 names to 77 distinct database subjects using the existing September 21 matrix, including separate brands, breeding programs and universities. Genetics Uruguay is a provisional organization requiring identity confirmation. Mario Aguas-Alvarado is a provisional person, with no inferred employer. Existing IDs and distinctions are retained. List creation is additive and starts unsubscribed; Digest subscription remains explicit. Digest list editing preserves these non-company members rather than silently dropping them.

## Fresh capture and practical limits

Capture makes one bounded public Google News RSS search per selected subject (up to 100), with at most five known names/aliases and four workers. It uses the shared collection lease, is registered as a manual pipeline and invokes no paid research model. Existing berry-industry qualification and explicit stock-quote filtering apply to fresh and retained unreviewed discoveries; reviewed publications remain intact. Source URLs retain the exact observed RSS story wrapper when Google does not supply a trustworthy publisher article URL. No publisher homepage is fabricated as an article URL.

Queries use the requested dates and each exported row independently passes inclusive publication-date validation. Undated results are excluded with visible notes. A successful search is not proof of exhaustive competitor coverage; absent subjects remain visible. This captures discovery headlines and snippets, not guaranteed complete article bodies or AI-researched summaries. Broad institutions and ambiguous short names can still need relevance review. Full body capture and publication/statement review remain existing, separate jobs. Jobs interrupted by a server restart require a fresh capture; this local worker is not a durable distributed queue.

Private jobs, captures and history live under `inbox/news_packets/`. Captures can appear in Personal Digest when the user subscribes to a matching list. They do not enter canonical Evidence or the public static build.

## JSON contract and receiving-system rules

`schemas/competitor-news-export.schema.json` defines conservative v1 types for the 28 field names supplied in the photo. The downloadable envelope contains `schema_version`, `batch_id`, `generated_at`, `scope`, `capture`, `records`, `validation`, `change_history` and `import_policy`. Every record includes all 28 supplied fields; absent event/access/import dates, confidence and related entities are nullable. Publication and event dates are distinct. Arrays preserve berry, region, country and topic selections without comma-joined values. UTC timestamps use ISO 8601. Separate competitor/related-mention rows retain the same `external_record_id` and `source_system`; co-mentions do not claim corporate relationships.

`record_status=approved` and `verification_status=source_reviewed` describe publication review only. Summaries retain source attribution and cannot be treated as verified Facts. Stable source IDs are primary deduplication keys; normalized URLs are only comparison keys, never replacements for emitted URLs. Headline plus date produces a possible-duplicate warning without collapsing distinct publications. Unknown related IDs and provisional competitor identities appear in exception output.

A CSV template and spreadsheet-safe validation/exception CSV are downloadable. This mission implements outgoing capture, preview, validation and immutable export history. It **does not implement a new receiving-system importer**. The supplied import policy must be implemented by the recipient: preview → validation → exception resolution → upsert commit; unknown identities held for review; approved plus explicitly enabled `publish_to_landscape=true` required before Landscape publication; batch validation and change history retained. The recipient's acceptance of these proposed JSON types/enums is still to be confirmed. No automatic import or publication is claimed here.
