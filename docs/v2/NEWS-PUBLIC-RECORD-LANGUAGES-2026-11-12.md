# Public-record and local-language News coverage

The next bounded expansion adds three direct official routes to the existing priority rotation: Serbia STIPS Agroinfo RSS, France RappelConso food-category RSS, and the UK Food Standards Agency's free structured alerts API. Priority routes increase from 48 to 51; the 12 priority and six regional checks per refresh, existing cadence, runtime lock and human trust gates remain unchanged. No paid provider is invoked and no Source registry record is approved or created.

The UK adapter retains original alert titles, product names and risk statements. Product detail can establish an explicit berry subject when an original title says only “fruit products.” It accepts published records with an original created day and a verified HTTPS official alert URL; modified dates never replace missing publication dates. It caps the response at two MB and 100 records, has a 12-second timeout and follows no redirects or nested links. Created day is publisher metadata, not independently verified first-public visibility. Collection Ops identifies the transport as Official JSON; live records retain the official identifier and date basis.

The June 23, 2026 Prepworld fruit-product recall is a consumed historical adapter control: strawberry appears in publisher product details, not the headline. It is outside the current 30-day News acquisition window and receives no prospective recall credit. The latest API response contained 100 records with November 10 freshness; one berry-qualified historical control survived relevance. Archive completeness is unproven.

The November 5 STIPS NALED export-potential report provides an actual Serbian summary-only crop control: maline and kupine identify raspberry and blackberry. The source attributes an analysis to NALED; ministry hosting does not make its reported projections an administrative finding or prove realized exports. The feed returned ten entries through November 10 and one qualifying story. Full original wording, dates and attribution remain retained. The known pre-window control is not a new independent pilot win.

Shared crop vocabulary covers Serbian Latin and Cyrillic forms and unambiguous Vietnamese names for all four crops. Native production, harvest, trade and risk terms support both collection and default News projection. Two rotating regional queries add Serbia and Vietnam without expanding the per-refresh request budget. Vietnamese dâu alone (also mulberry), generic mâm xôi without a distinguishing crop term, and French mûre alone (also “ripe”) do not assign a target crop. Serbian/Vietnamese recipe controls remain excluded; French qualified blackberry-food phrases and food-pathogen topics are recognized.

French RappelConso returned 20 dated current nonberry food notices and zero eligible berry items. Its shallow food-category window is explicit. No filtering behavior is assumed from an undocumented keyword parameter: a tested parameter returned the unfiltered page. Historical archive coverage remains open.

Five candidate indexed routes (Australia Fair Work, Chile SAG, Peru's official portal, RTV and Politika) returned no qualifying candidates during this check and remain unactivated. The Australian headline lacks crop identity in index metadata and permitted primary-body retrieval previously timed out. The old SENASA Contigo feed is reachable but ends in December 2023 and remains inactive. Generic search matches and successful transport do not establish effective coverage. Current archive/body routes need further verification; these gaps remain visible rather than being counted as covered.

Existing eight offline translation model languages are preserved. Serbian has no direct English package in the checked Argos index; new unsupported-language originals remain visible, not silently dropped or marked as verified translations. Vietnamese has an upstream model candidate but this release does not install or qualify it. Neither crop recognition nor translation-guard acceptance establishes semantic translation accuracy.
