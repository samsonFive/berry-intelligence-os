# Three real teaching photographs in Learn

Plasticulture, spotted-wing drosophila and Botrytis gain source-linked teaching
photos alongside their existing interactive explanations. Captions point out
what to inspect and the limits of the image. Creator, file-specific reuse basis
and source links stay beside each photograph. These are educational examples,
not cultivar authentication, farm diagnoses or instructions for pesticide use.

The file pages and actual images were read on November 4, 2026:

app uses Commons' supplied 330px thumbnail, without further image editing.

loaded and link to the complete source/reuse record.
