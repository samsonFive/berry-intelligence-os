# Berry OS — visual and interaction acceptance

**Revision 3, 11 November 2026. Status: accepted design direction; checkpoint planned, not run.** No implementation, dependency installation, provider call, data generation/change or deployment is authorized by this document.

This refines the existing consolidation plan using the user-supplied [design review addendum](DESIGN_REVIEW_ADDENDUM.md). It preserves the four-destination IA and the state, evidence, persistence, API, ownership and implementation contracts in [the proposal](BERRY_OS_EXPERIENCE_CONSOLIDATION_PROPOSAL.md), [frontend contracts](FRONTEND_CONTRACTS.md) and [agent workstreams](COORDINATED_AGENT_WORKSTREAMS.md). It is experience acceptance, not another repository or production audit.

## 1. Accepted direction

**Landscape · Briefing · Workspaces · Learn** is accepted as the primary information architecture. Directories, dossiers, portfolios, maps, comparisons and evidence support an investigation. Secondary review/curation/monitoring/operations retain their distinct effects and access. Follow, focus, comparison membership and evidence approval remain separate actions.

The landing Landscape must reveal meaningful relationships before the first interaction. Fewer menu entries, a new component library or a successfully mounted SDK does not establish that outcome.

## 2. Default Landscape composition

Use the three agreed modules with an explicit hierarchy:

1. **Scope readout:** two or three short findings naming actors, activities or relationships supported by the applied scope. Keep counts, missing-field messages and unsupported strategic conclusions out of the lead. Focus and detail changes do not redefine this scope summary. Only an applied-scope change changes its scope; development-derived statements retain the already-declared publication-window applicability.
2. **Main analytical field:** the visual center. Default to compact aligned relationship bands exposing real company → program → variety paths where explicit records support each link, with role/direction and concise geographic activity. Separate source-named cultivars from approved portfolio roles. Geography must make differences such as validation, planned entry, registered office and operating/growing activity intelligible at rest. An intentional matrix may replace a band composition if it explains the actual relationships better; neither is a roster decorated with cards or an arbitrary network.
3. **Relevant developments:** a small subordinate set of existing visual News cards. Use the deterministic relevance precedence below, with access to the full scoped Briefing. Never place an unfiltered general feed under every investigation.

Connections, Portfolio, Geography and Comparison replace the same main field. Sensory demo is another explicitly labeled main-field state. They do not append duplicate dashboards, reset selections or create competing full-page tabs. A compact scope bar and one command area precede the intelligence; advanced filter/layout/operational controls stay intentional.

### Before-interaction test

Using current authorized populated records, a reviewer must be able to explain a meaningful connection and an actual distinction before clicking: which actor connects to a program or cultivar, in what recorded role, and what the selected geographic activity means. For the earlier audited cohort, the candidate contrast is Fall Creek's reported validation/planned-entry context versus Costa's reported growing/licensing context. These are prior examples to recheck, not fresh production assertions. Do not invent a missing edge, promote a source-named association or strengthen a claim to complete the composition.

A plain organization list beside news, a generic card wall, a KPI wall or unnamed connecting lines fails this test. Precision and readability take priority over the number of entities displayed.

## 3. Development relevance precedence

Apply existing module scope rules, then choose the rail's subject in this order:

1. Active Comparison lens with a valid typed cohort → that cohort.
2. Otherwise an explicit in-scope focus → that focal entity.
3. Otherwise → the applied scope.

The heading names the active subject basis. Hover, relationship/source inspection and opening a descendant detail do not retarget the rail by themselves. Pinned subjects do not take over the rail until the Comparison lens is active. Outside-scope inspection does not enlarge the base landscape or its scoped developments; beyond-scope material can appear in clearly labeled contextual detail under the existing contract.

Use actual entity/source links and the existing date/geographic semantics. Co-mentions remain labeled. If no records match the chosen subject, show one honest empty state and an action to open the full scoped Briefing. Do not silently substitute general news, widen filters or imply that no real-world developments exist.

This is a rendering/relevance policy over the existing contract; it adds no new scope field, store or trust inference.

## 4. One coordinated detail area

Company, variety, relationship, observation, source and Copilot output share coordinated screen space. On desktop, detail may resize the analytical field while preserving readability. Narrow layouts deliberately enter a focused detail state with an explicit return. Full dossiers/readers remain available for deeper work.

Preserve ancestor detail, scope, cohorts, lens, scroll and keyboard focus through repeated source inspection. Avoid a permanent chat sidebar plus company drawer plus evidence drawer. Copilot's command opens or updates the same comparison/main-field/detail components used by direct controls; chat may acknowledge or explain the action, but must not render a duplicate investigation beside it.

Hover/focus supplies enterable support without changing scope, pin membership, URL/history or starting research. The name, relationship, cell or score is the trigger. Click/tap and keyboard equivalents open persistent detail. Material roles/activity/status remain visible at rest; excerpts, methodology, confidence, review history and fuller context are inspectable. Missing observations remain quiet unknowns, never zero. Research coverage remains a deliberate workflow.

## 5. Visual direction

Use warm white/ivory surfaces, dark botanical text, selective sage/forest accents and restrained berry colors. Differentiate editorial findings, entity identity, analytic values and controls through typography/composition, not repeated tinted boxes. Keep important company/variety names and findings readable; configuration controls and secondary metadata carry less visual weight.

Permitted fruit/growing/company imagery can add meaning, particularly in existing News cards. Avoid decorative agricultural heroes, invented cultivar photography and vendor-default themes. AG Grid and Copilot components consume the same Berry tokens and interaction primitives. SDK proof and visual quality are independent acceptance questions.

The grid remains purposeful for expanded portfolio analysis and demanding tables. Small semantic tables remain useful. The default report must not be the old spreadsheet with a different grid engine.

## 6. Sensory demonstration

Retain the isolated synthetic fixture, fictitious specimen IDs and 1–9 scale, most scores 4–7, to be generated only during later separately authorized implementation.

Default to a readable labeled dot comparison with a visible 1–9 axis. Name the one metric deliberately—for example **synthetic overall liking** if that is the chosen fixture variable. Do not substitute sweetness intensity, acidity intensity and liking for each other. Additional dimensions require deliberate schema definitions. A distribution view is optional only when the synthetic schema includes observations/replicates that support it; do not manufacture consumer distributions from one value per specimen.

One prominent synthetic label on every self-contained panel is sufficient; fuller context is available on hover/focus/click. Standalone outputs carry their own label. The demo uses the same visual system but remains outside canonical facts, ordinary search, coverage counts and standard exports. An explicit labeled annex remains separate. No score is attached as measured performance of a real commercial cultivar.

## 7. V1 — visual and interaction checkpoint

**Position:** after G1 technical adoption proof and the first populated Landscape composition, including its early isolated sensory state; **before propagating its visual/interaction patterns across Briefing, Workspaces and Learn**. Engineering gates G0, G1 and G2 keep their existing meaning. V1 is an additional experience gate.

W7 contributes the isolated demo early; its full output/Workspace/Learn work follows V1. Schedule that early contribution in an available slot after a bounded W4–W6 handoff; keep the limit of three implementation agents plus the lead. Foundational APIs/build/auth and independent tests can proceed; cross-surface pattern rollout waits for experience acceptance.

Before recording, recheck only the proposed current authorized scenario records and sources. Earlier screenshots do not establish current production content. If the candidate relationships are missing or insufficient, record that limitation and choose a supported scenario transparently. Do not manufacture records, strengthen claims or initiate a broad audit.

### Evidence package

- Five populated desktop screenshots covering the states above.
- One short continuous interaction recording covering scope → company/variety/relationship → comparison → development/evidence → return; include the same-investigation Copilot comparison action. The sensory transition can be included in the same recording or a clearly labeled continuation.
- A narrower-layout review plus keyboard-only and touch-equivalent access to preview/detail/return; verify reduced-motion behavior.
- A context-restoration check, and save/reload in Landscape when available without broader pattern rollout. Full native Workspace reopen and final PDF remain G2 requirements.
- A concise pass/rework record naming record/source IDs used, filters, relevant application revision, reviewer, unresolved defects and owner assignments. Include no credentials/account values/private employer material in review artifacts.

Attractive isolated components, fixture-only SDK calls and engineering test success do not establish V1. The packet must demonstrate a populated, continuous investigation.

### Ownership and disposition

- **W2** owns visual hierarchy, tokens, preview primitives and design review recommendation.
- **W4** owns relationship-visible-at-rest composition and analytic-view fixes.
- **W5** owns coordinated detail/Reader behavior and focus/return fixes.
- **W6** owns Copilot operating the same investigation without a duplicate canvas.
- **W7** owns early synthetic state and later outputs.
- **W8** produces/checks the evidence packet and records reproducible behavior defects.
- **W0** records pass/rework and enforces the dependency before cross-surface pattern propagation, using the design collaborator/user review when available.

These are existing owners, not new parallel agents or a separate theme/state implementation. Return defects to their owners. A failed V1 holds pattern rollout, then repeats the relevant states and continuous sequence after fixes. Independent unaffected engineering can continue. Passing V1 does not authorize deployment or replace G2's state/save/auth/export/production checks. User design feedback overrides an automated visual pass.
