## A short investigation

The evening design revision adds a dense monitored-company starting point and a
Landscape News view. For the isolated ten-company review, use
`/app/landscape?ctx_v=1&berry=all&countries=&company_set=watched`.
Production Watch membership is not seeded by this prototype. “Shared watched
companies” reads the existing workspace Watch list; it does not assert that
collection is active. “All companies” exposes the full company universe through
pages rather than a fixed ten-company limit. Compare pins remain separate.
Open the News tab to scan retained cards for that company set, then open a source
in the same Reader. The feed retains berry, source geography and publication-date
filters; focus and comparison pins do not retarget it. Empty watched sets stay
empty. Older saved investigations and URLs default to All companies.

1. Start with the three findings and visible connections. Explain the recorded
   distinction between Fall Creek's validation/planned-entry context and Costa's
   growing/licensing context. Company presence does not establish cultivar growing.
2. Select Fall Creek. Inspect a role or relationship, follow its citation, then
   return. Scope and comparison pins remain separate from the selected company.
3. Compare Fall Creek and Costa in China. Direct controls and the verified command
   **“Compare Fall Creek and Costa in China”** operate the same comparison field.
   The bounded demonstration runtime admits this exact command for one session;
   a fresh runtime is needed for another rehearsal. Navigation makes no model turn.
4. Open the Costa BluGenix development. Its retained source is dated June 8, 2026.
   Use Article when retained text is available, or Brief for the retained summary.
   Return to the same comparison. Publication review and individual claim approval
   remain separate.
5. Switch to Sensory demo. The specimens and overall-liking values are fictitious.
   Show the full 1–9 axis; the real company/cultivar pins remain intact.
6. Open Workspaces. Save a new investigation, reopen it, then preview a dated PDF.
   The preview lists complete row/source counts, including rows beyond the screen.
   The synthetic annex is an explicit separate choice and is off by default.
7. Open Learn and select Firmness. Show why a measurement needs its method, then
   return to the library. Its educational diagrams and sources retain their own
   meaning; they are not competitive Evidence or company performance scores.

## Output meaning

* A saved investigation preserves selectors and resolves current authorized data
  when reopened.
* Existing Brief Packs preserve a live composition. They are not frozen reports.
* A downloaded PDF carries its generation date, selected scope and source appendix.
* Authored Workspaces and Learn editing/research/version controls remain available
  through their existing governed pages, with a return to the investigation.

If a provider is unavailable, use the direct comparison controls. The actual
funded comparison proof and final unpaid interaction recording are retained in
the local acceptance artifacts. Source inspection reads retained material.
