# Product Vision

## Mission

Enable analysts to transform public information, observations, reports, and organizational knowledge into trusted, explainable intelligence about the global berry marketplace.

## North-star outcome

A product leader should be able to understand:

- where a market entity sits within the competitive landscape;
- where challenges and emerging threats exist;
- how each berry category is evolving globally;
- which competitors, varieties, traits, sources, and markets matter;
- what deserves reading, testing, commercial review, or continued monitoring;
- why the platform reached a conclusion.

The platform should also be useful as an onboarding environment for global product and commercial teams.

## Product identity

Berry Intelligence OS is not a passive document repository and not merely a news aggregator. It is an evidence-first intelligence workstation that turns source material into connected, reviewable, decision-relevant knowledge.

## Vendor neutrality

The platform must never assume or encode the operator’s employer as a privileged organization. Every company, breeder, nursery, grower, retailer, university, and market participant is represented through the same entity model. Any organization-specific perspective must be an optional, local configuration—not a hard-coded product assumption.
