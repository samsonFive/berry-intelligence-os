# Rijk Zwaan berry filing references

The 2021 CPVO Gazette lists two `Fragaria vesca` applications and names Rijk
Zwaan at party code 00088. Rendered PDF pages 35 and 193 confirm the references
and party. The record retains the lowercase spelling from the Gazette.

Mexico lists one raspberry in two stages. The March 2025 notice records
application 4330 and the June notice records its filing certificate 3563.
Both notices cite `97-VR0001 RZ` and are linked to the same variety candidate.
The notices were published May 7 and August 1, 2025, respectively.
