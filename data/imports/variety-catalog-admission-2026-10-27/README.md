# Selective source-backed catalog admission

The user explicitly authorized promotion of clear, source-backed identities while retaining ambiguous cases. The fixed 1,125-name audit admitted 432 identities and held 693. This brings the cleaned catalog from 62 to 494 entries.

Admission requires a literal name, established berry, a public primary/technical source, distinct identity, no existing name/code/register match, no shared name/code/commercial surface with another candidate of the same berry, and no digit-bearing selection name needing separate code review. Accepted source tiers are registry, national register, breeder catalog, patent/PVR and technical sheet. Source-backed here refers to the existing dated source observations/imports; it does not claim that every website was freshly fetched or that every cultivar is currently marketed.

Every new entity remains `unverified` and records the source URL, source label, candidate ID, reviewer attribution and delegated authorization. The audit adds **no aliases, Facts, Evidence links, company relationships, agronomic traits, current legal status or growing footprints**. The manifest lists the admitted IDs and the explicit reasons for held names. It is not a bulk claim-review approval. Remaining weak leads, coded selections, collisions and unresolved associations remain in review.

New entries were also copied into the isolated local preview. Production was not changed, and deployment remains held. Catalog removal is reversible and preserves source/history references. This admission expands identity inventory; it does not close the source-to-profile field-coverage gap for the newly admitted entries.
