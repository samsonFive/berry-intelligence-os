The five original single-page sheets were downloaded and visually checked on
November 5, 2026. Each sheet adds one source occurrence for the cultivar named
in its heading. Comparators, document filenames and chart labels do not add
cultivar names. [BluGenix variety page](https://blugenix.com.au/varieties/)

The figures remain attributed to their versions. The sheets give storage
temperature, but the records do not establish a comparable test method or
sample across cultivars. The Bounty PDF shows Central Mexico activity in January
through May while the web chart leaves that row blank. Both charts are leads
for growing-region review. [Produce Report launch article](https://www.producereport.com/article/costa-launches-blugenix-5-blueberry-varieties-suited-yunnan)

The five sources enter Coverage and the existing identity review. The linked
PDFs retain their imagery at the publisher. Photo reuse, variety identities,
structured traits and operating or growing regions retain their review steps.
