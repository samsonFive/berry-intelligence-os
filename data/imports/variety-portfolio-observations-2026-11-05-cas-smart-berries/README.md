# Original blueberry sources for CAS and Smart Berries

The Chinese forestry authority's second 2019 grant notice identifies 蓝闺蜜 and the Institute of Botany in row 197 on PDF page 11. The table prints application 20180703, filed October 22, 2018, and grant 20190411. The decision is dated December 31, 2019 and its web notice was posted January 8, 2020. The institute's own register table repeats the name, grant number and decision date. Current legal status, later transfers and commercialization remain open. [Grant notice](https://www.forestry.gov.cn/c/www/gkzfwj/272633.jhtml) · [Original attachment](https://www.forestry.gov.cn/html/main/main_72/20191226155119210120406/file/20200108102428229433872.pdf) · [Institute listing](https://pdsc.ibcas.ac.cn/cgry/pz/xwzq/)

Wendy Morris's Spring 2026 Australian Berry Journal field report names Magica as established production, Sekoya Pop as a trial and Sekoya Beauty as a recent addition with a first commercial harvest expected in 2027. These are dated observations about the Smart Berries Crow’s Nest site in Queensland. The caption identifies a Magica photograph by Wendy Morris on printed page 106. Other captions identify Sekoya Pop on pages 105 and 107. The report is linked for attribution and reuse review. [Original field report](https://berries.net.au/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/ABJ-Spring-2026-Edition-28-BB-%E2%80%98Sekoya-Pop-A-Crunchy-Revolution.pdf)

Both sources enter the review workflow with their printed names and original links. The earlier CAS strawberry research-material and Smart Berries brand-only records retain their limited scopes. The new name occurrences do not update rights, roles, regional footprints or photo permissions.
