# University of Arkansas 2024 blueberry trial — name observations

The [original November 22, 2024 report](https://www.uaex.uada.edu/farm-ranch/crops-commercial-horticulture/horticulture/ar-fruit-veg-nut-update-blog/posts/2024-blueberry-variety-trial.aspx)
puts its full twenty-variety list in an image. The original Table 1 was visually
read on October 9, 2026: five Northern Highbush, four Southern Highbush and eleven
Rabbiteye names. All twenty literal names are retained, with no exclusions.

This is a historical trial list, not a list of varieties bred, owned or licensed
by the university. No trait, yield, harvest date, growing-region or identity
claim is promoted. The twenty names enter the existing private candidate
reconciliation and human identity review; canonical varieties are unchanged.
The image is linked as the source, not copied into hosted/public assets, and
no image reuse permission is inferred.

The report's prose was originally missed because its publisher HTML wraps it
in a `nav` element. The bounded acquisition fix recovers that prose separately
as a private pending Source Fidelity artifact. Prose acquisition is not OCR
and cannot be counted as complete enumeration of an image table. The independent
visual enumeration here covers only Table 1. The separate current university
breeding listing, which names Norman, remains intact; it is not expanded to
include these twenty trial entries or treated as complete licensing/history.
