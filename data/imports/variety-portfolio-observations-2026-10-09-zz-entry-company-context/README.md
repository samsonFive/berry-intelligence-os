# Entry-level company source context

This additive snapshot revises the same eight register source IDs without
changing their names, page links, accounting or earlier file bytes. The latest
loader retains their original ordering and candidate identities.

148 of 388 observations now carry an explicit organization named in their
original Origin passage, mapped to 18 existing company/program identities.
240 remain without an assignment: addenda, missing passages, unresolved
organizations and unclear extraction spellings. Cultivar code prefixes are
never used to infer companies. Historical agencies, parent companies and
commercial/public institute identities are not collapsed into one another.

`source_company_ids` and `source_company_context` are unreviewed provenance.
They support company filtering and source-name handoffs, not approved breeder,
owner, licensee or other roles. Existing source-wide association remains the
fallback for older single-program snapshots; an explicit empty entry list
means no entry association. No canonical edits or human decisions are made.
