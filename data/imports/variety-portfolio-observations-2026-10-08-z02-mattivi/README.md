# Mattivi original named variety pages

Twelve original portfolio/detail pages checked October 8, 2026 provide sixteen
named occurrences for eight distinct offered labels across four berries.
Raspberry, strawberry, blueberry and blackberry portfolio bodies were read in
the native browser. All detail pages were fully read; the blackberry detail
required the native browser after a web-reader cache miss.

Six source name/code pairs are retained in display labels and context. Codes
are excluded from separate cultivar counts; no official denomination, breeder
code, alias or PBR status is inferred from a marketing heading. The blueberry
detail's Duke maturity comparator is excluded from Mattivi's offering list.
Karima/Munira page titles also use AuroraKarima/AuroraMunira, while their body
headings differ. Blackberry's index has a mismatched browser title; visible
headings and the linked original detail establish the crop context.

Four original raspberry portfolio-card photos were visually checked and are
credited to Mattivi Group with source links. Their associations remain proposed
and reuse is unknown: hidden by default, session preview only, no public output.
Generic header fruit, logos, decorative images and unverified detail images are
not assigned. Source PBR/quality/yield claims establish neither granted rights
nor independently reviewed performance. Copyright, file dates and retrieval
dates are not release or publication dates.

The z02 suffix appends after existing same-day observations, preserving earlier
candidate anchors. All prior candidate IDs and human decisions must remain
stable. These observations do not create canonical varieties, relationships,
footprints, approved traits, rights, reviewed evidence or photo permissions.
