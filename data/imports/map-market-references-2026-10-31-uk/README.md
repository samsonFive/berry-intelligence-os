# Defra fruit-table capture for review

Three UK crop rows and nine figures from the official 2025 horticulture dataset,
published June 25, 2026 and captured October 31. The JSON retains original cell
texts and cached numeric values alongside source-linked proposals. Complete
ODS and HTML responses remain in the private capture with their SHA-256 hashes.
No raw workbook is published, and no source reference is automatically selected.

Planted area, marketed production and reported yield keep original units,
rounding and provisional qualifications. Strawberry/raspberry area differences
between workbook and narrative remain explicit. Yield is never calculated;
other soft fruit is never relabeled blackberry. The public reference seed is
unchanged. See docs/v2/MAP-UK-OFFICIAL-REFERENCES.md for methods, direct sources,
review boundaries and current verification. Source: Defra / Crown copyright,
Open Government Licence v3.0 as provided on the GOV.UK publication page.
