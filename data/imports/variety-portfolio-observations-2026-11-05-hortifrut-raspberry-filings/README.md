# Hortifrut raspberry patents and Mexican breeder rights

Hortifrut’s genetics page prints **Pacific Scarlet**. The U.S. patent, the
producer’s 2022 report and two Mexican notices use **Pacific Starlet**.
The catalog keeps the existing Scarlet record and collects Starlet’s four
sources under a separate identity review. Pacific Gema matches the existing
catalog name. Its rights records remain source observations.

any Scarlet/Starlet relationship, photo reuse and growing regions require
