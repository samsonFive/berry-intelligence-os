# Well-Pict: historical printed strawberry labels

The original [March 2012 Produce Business issue, page 68](https://producebusiness.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/pb-mar-2012.pdf#page=68)
prints **#3352** and **#4053** in its Well-Pict-branded merchandising profile.
Both labels occur in the strawberry supply paragraph describing 2011. This adds
one bounded, unreviewed historical source section; it does not close the current
company-site gap or establish a complete portfolio.

The original 152-page, 9,917,469-byte PDF returned HTTP 200. Its SHA-256 is
`44fa3d6e11c3d070e3cd97acf42550c4f0d4431345305dfef1675507d37654b3`.
Page 68 was extracted, rendered with Poppler and visually checked against its
printed heading, labels and March 2012 footer. Capture time is retained in UTC;
the check date is November 3 in the operator's local timezone. No exact
publication day is invented, and the upload directory does not date the issue.

The numeric labels are retained literally. No denomination, breeder-code prefix,
alias, company role, current right, supply status, verified trait or growing
footprint is inferred. The page's generic field, food and pack images do not
identify either label, so no variety photograph is assigned. Existing patent
observations for PSI-.118 and PSI-130 remain separate; no mapping is asserted.

Only body-free factual source metadata and short paraphrases enter this import.
The PDF, raw extraction, rendered research image and contact details remain in
ignored local storage. Canonical records, private user fields, human decisions,
review gates and provider configuration are unchanged.
