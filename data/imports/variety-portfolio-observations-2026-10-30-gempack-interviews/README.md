# Gem-Pack interview names

Three unreviewed scopes retain ten literal name occurrences from two original interviews. FreshPlaza, July 21, 2026, names Alturas and Carpinteria strawberries and Majestic and Adelita raspberries. The Packer, April 7, 2025, printed page32/PDF page34, names Fronteras, Monterey, Belvedere, Victor, Adelanto and San Andreas strawberries. The public newspaper copy is hosted by UnitedAg; its filename date is not the publication date.

The full July article and relevant newspaper page were read; the newspaper page was rendered and visually checked. Adjacent Western Veg-Produce material is excluded. Reprints do not add independent sources. Images lack individual cultivar identification and remain unassigned. Raw captures stay private. These are source associations, not approved company roles, identities, growing regions, traits or rights. Original canonical records, saved decisions and other incomplete Gem-Pack checks remain.
