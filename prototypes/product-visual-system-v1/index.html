Desktop, tablet, mobile

Use the viewport toolbar. Rail stacks on small screens; drawers go full-bleed; touch targets stay ≥44px.

  • Desktop — rail + content; sticky filters; reader drawer ~36rem
  • Tablet — condensed rail or top nav; two-column cards collapse thoughtfully
  • Mobile — single column; no horizontal trap; full-screen reader
