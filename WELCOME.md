# Welcome to Berry Intelligence OS

Berry Intelligence OS exists because competitive intelligence should not disappear into presentations, email threads, spreadsheets, or individual memory.

Every observation should strengthen the shared understanding of the market. Every insight should remain explainable. Every recommendation should be supported by evidence.

The purpose of this project is not to collect information. Its purpose is to create understanding.

## Product vision

A global product leader should be able to use the platform and say:

> “I know where we are in the marketplace and where we face challenges. I can find out about each berry and the playing field on a global scale. I could use this to onboard my global teams.”

## Core principles

1. Evidence before conclusions.
2. Facts are separate from interpretation.
3. Interpretation is separate from recommendation.
4. Every published conclusion is traceable.
5. AI proposes; a human approves.
6. JSON is authoritative.
7. Generated indexes and pages are disposable.
8. Local-first and portable by default.
9. Market entities are treated neutrally.
10. Knowledge should compound over time.
